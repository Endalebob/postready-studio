Docker Compose runs Next.js and FastAPI. No Redis, Celery, database, or business accounts in this demo.

Follow one advertisement

  1. Upload: keep the photo in browser memory and show a preview.
  2. Enter details: name, current price, contact required; description, address, old price optional. Choose language.
  3. Generate: send a multipart request; show indeterminate progress while FastAPI calls Gemini.
  4. Compose: receive text-free artwork and structured copy; draw them together in a 1080 × 1080 canvas.
  5. Edit: image text and social caption change independently. Artwork remains unchanged.
  6. Download: use the same renderer for preview and PNG export after fonts load.
Journey UI · Screens and retained work

The top-level Next.js client holds the file, details, language, result, edits, and errors. Step changes and generation failures retain these values; closing or reloading clears them.

Files: frontend/app/page.tsx, frontend/components/, frontend/lib/api.ts.

PRD: Screens and Layout; States and Boundaries.

Validation · Prices, contacts, and uploads

Require product name, positive current price, and one to four phone/social contacts, with add/remove controls. Old price must exceed current price. Calculate discount from both prices; omit it when old price is absent.

Proposed demo defaults: ETB currency, JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MiB and 20 decoded megapixels. Invalid values get clear field messages.

Files: backend/app/schemas.py, backend/app/images.py, frontend/lib/validation.ts.

PRD: Product and Business Details; Photo Upload.

Generation · Live artwork and copy

POST /generate accepts the photo and JSON details. Return artwork bytes, image headline/tagline, a separate social caption, authoritative details, and calculated discount.

Use the official Google SDK. Use original Nano Banana for images and Gemini 3.8 Flash for copy, with configurable model names. Live requests in both languages have succeeded. No simulated generation substitutes for the live demo.

Files: backend/app/main.py, backend/app/gemini.py.

PRD: Language Choice; Advertisement Generation.

Renderer · Editable text that exports correctly

One canvas renderer draws artwork and readable text blocks. Wait for locally bundled Noto Sans Ethiopic, wrap text, and surface overflow rather than clipping. Editing social copy never changes image words.

Files: frontend/lib/render-ad.ts, frontend/components/ResultEditor.tsx, frontend/public/fonts/.

PRD: Caption Editing and Download; Look and Feel.

Failure recovery · Bounded and visible

Retry a transient failure once, within a proposed 180-second total time budget. Bad credentials, invalid input, and policy refusals are not automatically retried.

If generation still fails, retain the draft and offer Retry. If export text does not fit, ask for shortening and preserve edits. A closed page does not create a resumable background job.

Docker Desktop, internet, and a backend Gemini key are needed. Put the key in ignored local configuration.
