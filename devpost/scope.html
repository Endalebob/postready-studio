Identity and learning

Your experiments with AI image generation and editing sparked the idea. Your learning goal is to make the app easy for business owners to use for advertising.

Ethiopian representation matters when future ads include people. The specific visual treatment for the first product-only version still needs definition in the product requirements step.

TikTok, Instagram, and Telegram are sharing destinations of interest. The approved scope uses downloads and leaves automatic posting for later.

Review notes

The core flow and downloadable result give this project a clear demonstration. Deferring people wearing products keeps the first experiment manageable. The visual meaning of Ethiopian cultural relevance and the exact ad layout still need sharpening in the next planning step.
