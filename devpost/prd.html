1 · Home

Explain the app, show inspirational example advertisements, and give the owner a prominent create-ad button.

First use: no profile or account required.

Check: the owner understands the photo-to-ad outcome and can start creating.

2 · Upload the product

Upload one product photo, then move to the details step. The shoe photo anchors the demo.

Unusable photo: explain the issue and allow replacement.

Check: a usable product photo advances; an unusable file receives a clear message.

3 · Details and language

Required: product name, current price, and one to four contact methods (phone or social account), with add/remove controls. Optional: description, shop address, and old price. Choose Amharic or English before generation. No business profile in this demo.

Old price must exceed current price; calculate the discount from the two prices. Without old price, show no discount. Invalid or missing required values get a clear field message before generation.

Check: the final image contains readable, accurate owner-provided business details.

4 · Generate and recover

Show generation in progress, then produce a clean product advertisement. Keep editable words separate from the artwork.

Failure: retry internally; if still unsuccessful, retain photo and details and offer Retry with a clear message. No promise to contact the owner.

Check: the original product remains recognizable; a failed attempt preserves entered work.

5 · Edit and download

Display the ad with separate editors for image text and social-post caption. Image-text edits change the ad while preserving artwork; social-caption edits are independent. Download the current edited ad.

Agreed: image text and social-post caption are separately editable.

Check: edit a phrase, download the result, and see that exact edit in the image with unchanged artwork.

Later: business profiles/accounts/sync, people wearing products, multiple design alternatives, and automatic social posting.
