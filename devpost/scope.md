# PostReady Studio

A mobile-friendly app that turns one product photo into a downloadable advertisement and an editable Amharic or English caption for Ethiopian small-business owners.

## The Unique Kernel
Make a product photo ready for local advertising by combining a clean, appealing product presentation with readable business details and Amharic or English copy. Ethiopian cultural relevance is the intended identity; its concrete visual treatment for the first version still needs definition in the PRD.

## Who It's For
An Ethiopian small-business owner preparing a product advertisement on a phone. Clothing shops, cosmetics shops, and restaurants are examples; the demonstration uses a shoe photo without committing to a shoe-only app. Their current advertising workflow has not been established.

## The Core Loop
Upload one product photo, provide the product and shop details, receive an ad image and caption, edit the caption, and download the image for sharing. Return when another product needs advertising. TikTok, Instagram, and Telegram are intended sharing destinations.

## Inspiration & Identity
Inspired by the learner's hands-on experiments with AI image generation and editing of attached images. The result should have a clean background and look good as an advertisement. The experience should be easy for business owners to use. Ethiopian representation matters when people appear in future ads; the learner's example is an Ethiopian person wearing an advertised shoe. People in the ad are deferred from the first demo. No specific reference tool or URL was supplied.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Help Ethiopian small businesses create advertising pictures easily. Practice making an app easy to use and useful enough to become their go-to advertising tool.

## What "Working" Looks Like
On a mobile-friendly screen, upload a shoe photo and enter price, shop address, and contact information, with discount information when relevant. Download an appealing advertisement with a clean background and those details visibly included. An editable caption is available in Amharic or English. The uploaded product should remain recognizable in the result.

The demonstration moment is seeing the original product photo become a ready-to-share advertisement containing the owner's details. Aim to demonstrate the flow in about a minute. The curriculum targets two to four hours of active work; submission requires a short demo video and public GitHub repository. Deployment is optional.

## The POC Boundary
- One product photo per advertisement.
- Owner-provided product and shop information: price, optional discount, address, and contact details.
- One clean advertisement image with the supplied details, downloadable by the owner.
- An editable Amharic or English caption.
- A mobile-friendly flow focused on ease of use.

## Later
- A person wearing or using the product, with Ethiopian representation: explicitly deferred by the learner.
- Multiple design alternatives and automatic social posting: deferred from the first proof of concept.

## Explicitly Cut
No additional permanent cuts established. The first demo stays focused on the upload-to-download loop; later features do not need to be implemented to prove it.
